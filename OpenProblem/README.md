# Formalization of Open Problems in Solid Mechanics

This directory follows selected research questions in solid mechanics, elasticity, and related PDEs. It uses existing problem collections as entry points and traces each selected question to its original statement and the literature that establishes partial or complete results.

The LeanSolid contribution is to express the assumptions and conclusions of selected problem variants in Lean. A future entry can link to a Lean proposition, cite the results that apply to it, and record whether the proposition has been stated in Lean or proved there. Literature status is recorded for the specific variant and dated independently of Lean verification.

Start with the [review of associated problem collections and sources](SOURCES.md). For paper-specific Lean developments, see [Research Companions](../ResearchCompanions/README.md). For classical results and their Lean declarations, see [Landmark Results](../LandmarkResults/README.md).
